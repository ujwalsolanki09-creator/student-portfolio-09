01 About me

I'm a Computer Science & AI student who likes both sides of the work: the logic behind a script and the detail behind a layout.

Most of my time goes into Python — small utilities, data handling, and problem sets that push me to think in structures rather than syntax. This web development course pulled me to the other end of the stack, where a page has to hold up on a phone as well as a wide monitor.

I don't copy tutorials and stop. I break the layout on purpose, change one property at a time, and read what the browser actually did. That habit is why this portfolio is hand-written HTML and CSS with no JavaScript anywhere in it.
